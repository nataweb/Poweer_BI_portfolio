Electric Vehicle Analysis Dashboard
An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing the U.S. electric vehicle population by vehicle type, model year, manufacturer, geographic distribution, CAFV eligibility, and vehicle model.
The dashboard provides key KPIs for BEV and PHEV populations and average electric range, while interactive slicers allow users to explore the data by city, electric utility, and vehicle type.
The analysis highlights trends in EV adoption across model years, geographic concentration across states, leading manufacturers and vehicle models, and the distribution of CAFV eligibility.
Key findings:
BEVs represent the largest share of the electric vehicle population.
EV volumes increase significantly across recent model years, with 2023 representing the highest volume in the dataset.
Washington has the highest number of EV records among the states shown.
Tesla is the dominant manufacturer in the dataset.
Tesla Model Y and Model 3 are the most represented vehicle models.
Most vehicles fall into the CAFV-eligible category, although eligibility status varies across the population.
